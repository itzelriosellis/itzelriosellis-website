Itzél Rios-Ellis

Itzél Rios-Ellis is an artist based in San Francisco, California. She has a BFA in Illustration from California College of the Arts. Engaging with a diverse array of mediums—including painting, digital and traditional illustration, fiber arts, printmaking, sculpture, and design — Rios-Ellis navigates multiple artistic avenues to convey distinct and evocative messages. Rios-Ellis's work particularly delves into the themes of the body and the notion of home. Through her art, she navigates these complex aspects of her life, creating a visual narrative that speaks to personal growth and the profound impact of these experiences on her artistic expression.
